01 — THE PROBLEM

Better generations.
Harder guarantees.

Reward guidance alone does not ensure a generative output satisfies hard requirements. ConicFlow formulates inference-time guidance as an optimal control problem: reward at the endpoint, constraints at the endpoint, and an energy penalty for departing from the pretrained flow.

CONTINUOUS-TIME CONSTRAINED INFERENCE
minᵤ ∫₀¹ ‖u(t)‖² / (2λ) dt − r(x₁)
subject to ẋₜ = bₜ(xₜ) + u(t),   h(x₁) ≤ 0
u(t): intervention · r: terminal reward · h: hard terminal requirements
02 — THE METHOD

Look ahead.
Control locally.

ConicFlow optimizes a short future window, executes the first step, and replans. Its terminal-rollout variant predicts the final sample through the pretrained flow map, keeping the true terminal reward and constraints in view.

Optimize path space

Avoid optimizing an entire generation trajectory at once. Replan over a tractable horizon.

Predict the endpoint

RH-T propagates a locally optimized endpoint using the pretrained flow map.

Learn constraint pressure

Projected primal–dual steps increase multipliers for violated constraints.

Execute and repeat

Apply the first optimized control action, shift the window, and warm-start the next problem.

Explore the two horizon formulations

RH-T evaluates reward and constraints at the predicted terminal point using the pretrained flow map.

04 — THE GUARANTEES

Provable control.
Practical horizons.

The analysis separates discretization error, horizon truncation, and finite primal–dual optimization error. Rates hold under the paper's stated structural and regularity assumptions.

Discretization

O(1/N)

Continuous-to-discrete optimal-value error under regularity and repairability conditions.

Horizon truncation

O((N−H)/N)

For constrained RH-H and RH-T under affine flow, convexity, and uniform feasibility.

Finite solver

O(1/√K)

Trajectory and constraint-violation bounds with sensitivity-dependent constants.
